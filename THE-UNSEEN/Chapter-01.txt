

 THE UNSEEN

By John Timbenao

 Chapter 1: The Boy Nobody Noticed

The school bell rang at exactly 7:30 in the morning.

Students rushed through the gates, laughing, shouting, and calling their friends' names. Some walked in groups, while others chased one another across the dusty school grounds.

Among them was a fourteen-year-old boy named Ntokozo.

He walked alone.

His schoolbag hung loosely over one shoulder. His shoes were old, and his uniform was always neatly arranged, even though it had started showing signs of wear.

Ntokozo wasn't an unfriendly boy. In fact, he wanted friends more than anything. He wanted someone to save him a seat, someone to ask how his weekend had been, and someone who would notice when he wasn't around.

But nobody ever did.

He entered his classroom quietly and walked towards the desk at the back.

"Hey, move! That's my friend's seat," a boy shouted.

Ntokozo stopped.

"Sorry," he whispered.

He picked up his bag and moved to another desk.

Nobody looked at him.

Nobody asked him where he wanted to sit.

And somehow, that hurt more than being insulted.

At break time, he sat beneath a tree near the classroom block. He opened his lunch container and watched the other students sharing food, telling jokes, and taking pictures.

He smiled when they laughed, even though he wasn't part of the conversation.

Sometimes, he wondered whether he was invisible.

When the final bell rang, Ntokozo walked home alone again.

His guardian was usually busy, and the house was rarely a place where he could talk freely about his feelings.

That evening, he opened an old notebook hidden beneath his mattress.

He turned to a clean page, held his pen for a moment, and wrote:

"I wonder if anyone would notice if I disappeared for a day."

He stared at the sentence.

Then he closed the notebook.

He didn't know it yet, but those words would become the beginning of a story nobody was prepared to hear.
