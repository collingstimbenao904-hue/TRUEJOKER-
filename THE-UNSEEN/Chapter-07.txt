

 THE UNSEEN

Chapter 7: The Day Everything Changed

Friday morning began with excitement.

The school was preparing for its annual sports competition. Students were running around the grounds, discussing teams, choosing activities, and making plans for the afternoon.

Ntokozo wasn't particularly interested in sports, but Sibusiso was excited.

"You're joining my team," Sibusiso announced.

Ntokozo laughed.

"I haven't even agreed."

"You don't have a choice. You're my friend."

Those words made Ntokozo smile.

You're my friend.

He had waited so long to hear something like that.

During the morning assembly, the head teacher announced that students would compete in different activities. Football, athletics, netball, and other games were on the schedule.

Sibusiso signed up for football.

He encouraged Ntokozo to join, but Ntokozo refused.

"I don't play football."

"Everyone plays football!"

"Not me."

Sibusiso laughed.

"Fine. At least come and support us."

Ntokozo agreed.

At lunchtime, the boys gathered near the football field. Sibusiso introduced Ntokozo to some of his classmates.

"This is my friend, Ntokozo."

A few greeted him.

One boy looked at Ntokozo's old shoes and laughed.

"That's your friend?"

Another joined in.

"Does he even know how to play football?"

Ntokozo forced a smile, pretending the comments didn't bother him.

Sibusiso immediately became serious.

"What's funny about that?"

"Relax, we're just joking," the boy replied.

But Ntokozo had already gone quiet.

He stepped away from the group and sat beneath a tree.

A few minutes later, Sibusiso found him.

"Why did you leave?"

"It's nothing."

"Ntokozo, they're just idiots. Don't listen to them."

Ntokozo looked at his friend.

"You don't have to defend me every time."

"Why not?"

"Because one day you'll get tired of it."

Sibusiso shook his head.

"That's not how friendship works."

Ntokozo didn't answer.

Later that afternoon, as students prepared to leave, something unexpected happened.

A teacher approached Ntokozo.

"Ntokozo, can you come to the staffroom tomorrow morning? There's something I would like to discuss with you."

Ntokozo became nervous.

"Did I do something wrong, sir?"

The teacher smiled.

"No. Don't worry. I just want to talk."

Ntokozo watched the teacher walk away.

For the rest of the afternoon, he wondered what the meeting could be about.

That evening, he opened his notebook and wrote:

"Today, I discovered that having a friend doesn't mean the world suddenly becomes kind. But perhaps it means I no longer have to face everything alone."

He closed the notebook.

Tomorrow, he would find out why the teacher wanted to see him.
