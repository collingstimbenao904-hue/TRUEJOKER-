



 Chapter 5: A New Beginning

The following morning, Ntokozo woke up earlier than usual.

For once, he wasn't dreading the journey to school.

He wondered whether Sibusiso would be there. He wondered whether they would sit together again. He even caught himself smiling while preparing his school uniform.

His guardian noticed him.

"What's making you so happy this morning?"

"Nothing," Ntokozo replied.

"Then why are you smiling?"

Ntokozo lowered his head.

"I just feel okay."

His guardian returned to what they were doing without asking anything further.

Ntokozo picked up his schoolbag and left.

When he arrived at school, he found Sibusiso standing near the classroom entrance.

"Ntokozo! You're early today."

"So are you," Ntokozo replied.

They laughed.

For the first time, walking into the classroom didn't feel like entering a place where he didn't belong.

During the morning lesson, Sibusiso borrowed his ruler, and they exchanged a few jokes. Their teacher even warned them to concentrate.

At break time, they sat beneath the same tree where Ntokozo used to eat alone.

Sibusiso shared his snacks with him.

"You know, I've been meaning to ask you something," Sibusiso said.

"What?"

"Do you ever invite people to your house?"

Ntokozo became quiet.

"Not really."

"Why?"

Ntokozo looked towards the football field.

"My home is complicated."

Sibusiso didn't laugh or ask another question.

Instead, he said, "That's okay. We don't have to talk about it."

Ntokozo appreciated that.

Not everyone needed to know everything about him.

As the days passed, the two boys became closer. They studied together, talked during break, and sometimes walked part of the way home.

Ntokozo was beginning to discover something he had almost forgotten: happiness could exist in ordinary moments.

But there was still one thing he hadn't told Sibusiso.

He was afraid.

Afraid that if someone discovered the truth about his life, they might leave him just like everyone else.

That evening, he opened his notebook.

"I think I have a friend now. But why am I still afraid of losing someone I have only just found?"

He closed the notebook.

For the first time, he had something to lose.
